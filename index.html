Vinyl prices and popularity

Today

The Vinyl Vault keeps an eye on 100 well-loved records so you don't have to. Every morning it checks Discogs, the marketplace where collectors around the world buy, sell, and show off their shelves. For each record it writes down five things: the lowest price right now, how many copies are for sale, how many people own it, how many still want one, and its average rating. Stack those days up and you can see which prices are climbing, which records are getting scarce, and which ones are catching on. Flip through Browse to search and star your favorites, or head to Trends for the bigger picture.
