What this site does

The Vinyl Vault is a price and popularity tracker for vinyl records. Every morning, on its own, it looks up 100 well-known albums on Discogs, the largest database of records and the place where collectors buy and sell them, and adds the day's numbers to a growing history.

For every record, every day, it tracks:

  • Lowest price - the cheapest copy currently for sale, in US dollars
  • Copies for sale - how many copies are listed right now
  • Collectors who have it and collectors who want it - a sign of how popular and how scarce a record is
  • Rating - the average of collectors' star ratings, and how many votes it rests on

From that history it works out which prices moved the most since yesterday, and which record is rising fastest in popularity for the day, week, month, year and decade (the record that gained the most collectors). Use Browse to filter, search, sort and star records, and Trends for charts and findings over time. The data updates by itself at about 3:00 AM Central.
